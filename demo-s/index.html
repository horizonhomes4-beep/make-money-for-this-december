Meet your guide

Hi, I’m Coach Reed 👋

Welcome to the free MathCloud Spelling demo. I am Coach Reed. I will say a word, and you type it on the on-screen keypad, so no spell checker can help you. You will never see the word while you work. If you need help, press Definition, Use in a sentence, or Origin, and I will read you the clue. Origin tells you which language the word comes from. This demo has three word registers with ten carefully chosen words each. Afterwards I will show you the correct spelling and walk you through every word you missed.

  • ⌨️ Type on a safe keypad
  • 💡 Definition, sentence, origin
  • 🗂️ Word registers by topic
  • 📈 Weak-register analysis
